# Gap review additions (2026-10-27)

> **Normative.** Split verbatim from the approved plan (`plan/P1-PLAN.md` §18, approved 2026-10-27). Changing anything here needs a new entry in the [decision log](../decisions.md).

These came from comparing the plan with FullCalendar, MUI X Scheduler, Bryntum, DHTMLX, Mobiscroll, Schedule-X, Google Calendar and Outlook.

**Added to 1.0**
